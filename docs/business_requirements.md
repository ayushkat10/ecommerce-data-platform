\# Enterprise Risk Data Platform



\## Objective



Build a banking-style data platform that simulates real-world Data Engineering workflows including:



\- Batch ingestion

\- Streaming ingestion

\- Data quality validation

\- Reconciliation

\- PII governance

\- Data freshness monitoring

\- RCA investigation

\- Regulatory reporting



\## Source Systems



1\. Customer Master

2\. Transaction System

3\. Exposure System

4\. Risk Metrics System



\## Planned Data Volumes



\- Customers: 10,000

\- Transactions: 100,000

\- Exposure Records: 20,000

\- Risk Metrics: 20,000



\## Incident Scenarios



1\. DV failures

2\. Missing records

3\. Count mismatch

4\. Kafka ingestion issues

5\. Late batch arrival

6\. Schema drift

7\. Upstream failures

8\. Data freshness failures

9\. PII violations

10\. Reconciliation failures
